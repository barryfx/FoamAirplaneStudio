# Fiberglass covering estimates

Wing, Fuselage, Horiz Stab and Vert Stab each have a Fiberglass secondary tool.
It is explicitly for Weight and Balance only. Patches never alter CAD solids,
manufacturing exports, readiness or component-generation fingerprints.

Add Fiberglass Shape selects a reusable SketchEditor layer. Draw one connected
chain of Lines/Splines, a closed loop, or a Circle. Escape finishes a spline.
The shape selector and editable fields store Name, Wrap Sides / One Sided,
Drawing surface, Cloth Weight, and resin thickness. Tools off permits selection,
point movement and individual curve deletion; Delete Fiberglass Shape removes
the whole patch. Independent patches may overlap: each is another cloth layer.
Teal outlines remain visible in 2D outside Fiberglass editing.

The covered region is the drawn shape intersected with the component outline.
An open chain is closed by joining its endpoints only if that joining segment
stays outside the component. A chain with an endpoint inside, a crossing closure,
branches or disconnected paths is retained for editing, but full weight/CG is
unavailable until corrected. A closed patch entirely outside has zero coverage.
Use another shape for each separate patch. Pending curves must be finished
before calculating coverage.

Wing and Horiz Stab use Top/Bottom and mirror patches onto both modeled halves.
Vert Stab uses Left/Right. Fuselage uses Top/Bottom on Top View and Left/Right
on Side View; this selector also specifies the projection for Wrap Sides.
One Sided counts the selected exposed face. Wrap Sides counts the exposed
surfaces on both sides and edge faces within the projected region. It does not
coat internal cavity walls or mating faces hidden by another body of that
component. Openings remain openings. Stabilizer controls are included with their
parent stabilizer. Separate components' overlapping patches remain independent.

Measurements use current placed/cut Assembly shapes. Private copies are meshed
at 0.1 mm linear / 0.15 rad angular deflection. Triangles are clipped against the
projected mask and their true 3D area and area centroid are accumulated. Edge-on
triangles are clipped as intervals in barycentric coordinates. Exterior visibility
is tested at clipped fragment centroids; curved surfaces and partially occluded
fragments are estimates, not exact laminate or manufacturing analysis. Wing
mapping follows each panel's unfolded frame and cumulative dihedral. Assembly
translation/rotation affects mass centers, not cloth area. No component is
generated by entering Fiberglass or calculating its mass.

Cloth defaults to 50 g/m². All four tabs, including their initial patches, follow
Reference units: g/m² for metric or oz/yd² for inches; switching units converts the displayed value without changing
physical cloth weight (1 oz/yd² = 33.9057474749 g/m²). Choosing another cloth unit stores a per-patch override; selecting the current
project unit resumes following Reference units. Navigation does not mutate data. Resin thickness follows Reference length units and can be
overridden by disabling the automatic estimate.

The default resin-only equivalent thickness is
`clothGm2 * (1/900 - 1/2550) + 0.01` mm. This assumes 900 kg/m³ bulk woven cloth,
2550 kg/m³ solid glass, fills the cloth void volume, and adds a 0.01 mm thin coat.
This is a starting estimate, not a universal weave/layup property. Changing cloth
weight updates automatic thickness; a manual thickness remains unchanged.
Material Densities in Weight and Balance edits foam, plywood, Carbon Fiber and
resin. Resin defaults to 1500 kg/m³ (1.5 g/cm³), as requested. Existing saved
density overrides remain unchanged. See ADR-0050 for the thickness assumptions.

For area A in mm², cloth mass is `A * clothGm2 * 1e-6` grams; resin volume is
`A * thicknessMm` mm³ and mass is `volume * resinDensityKgM3 * 1e-6` grams.
Both masses act at the covered area's centroid. The breakdown lists each patch's
area, cloth mass and resin mass, plus cloth/resin subtotals. Total mass, CG marker,
CG readout, shared statistics and wing loading include them.

Format 32 stores four sketch/metadata collections and resin density; formats
1–31 load empty covering collections and the default resin density. Save/Open,
New/Close, undo/redo and reference remapping include all patches. Selection and
idle drawing mode are view state. Coverage changes invalidate mass statistics,
not component solids. Density, cloth weight, thickness and name edits reuse
measured areas/centroids. Derived saved statistics also retain covering mass
inputs under the updated statistics key; stale data is never used as current.

Only Weight and Balance integrates mass or updates weight, wing loading and CG.
Every other mode displays dashes for these three statistics; outline dimensions
continue to update. Independent components/plywood inserts use bounded workers
for volume integration, and individual changed patches use up to 90% of reported
logical hardware threads (rounded down, minimum one) with
private meshes and classifiers. Results are reduced in component/patch order.
Inner covering meshes run serially to avoid nested worker multiplication.
Both cloth and resin rows show exactly the same covered area.

Each patch caches its measured area and centroid independently. Only misses are
scheduled, including concurrent patches on the same component. Boundary,
surface/wrap, relevant outline/projection, scale or source topology/placement
changes invalidate that entry; other patches and material-only edits reuse their
measurements. Reordering and removing patches preserves surviving cache hits.
These bounded caches last for the project session, reset on New/Open/Close, and
retain their previous entries if a calculation fails. See ADR-0051.

A Side View open U whose endpoints close below the fuselage covers the lower
left side, connecting bottom and lower right side in Wrap Sides mode, without
covering the top. One Sided covers only the selected side within that boundary.

Drawing over the other fuselage view opens a warning identifying the selected
Drawing Surface and expected view, plus the view containing the lines. Top and
Bottom map to Top View; Left and Right map to Side View. Warnings wait until a
drag ends and are suppressed while extending the same mismatched patch. Moving
the patch to the correct view clears the suppression. The other components have
a single outline projection shared by their opposing surfaces.
