Wing toolbar availability:
Outline is initially selected and is the only enabled Wing tool.
Defining Outline enables Airfoil Stations; defining stations enables Airfoils;
defining airfoils enables Dihedral. Its zero-degree panel defaults count as defined.
Defining Dihedral enables Ailerons/Flaps,
Spars, and Lightening together. Previously enabled tools remain editable.

Implemented panel details are maintained in docs/architecture: reference.md,
sketch-editor.md, airfoil-stations.md, airfoils.md, dihedral.md, wing-solids.md,
control-surfaces.md and spars.md. The drawing above includes planned editors;
it is not a claim that all illustrated tools/statistics are implemented.

Wing / Lightening
Descriptive text explains minimum walls, access split, solid controls/tip,
uniform crossmembers and unfolded span distances. Enable Lightening checkbox;
Wall Thickness; Number of Crossmembers; Crossmember Thickness; Start Distance
from Root; Stop Distance before Last Station. Physical entries accept mm/in
and retain entered text. Pins and sockets retain wall-thickness support.

Regeneration: wing panels run as independent, bounded background tasks. Disable
menus, shortcuts, toolbars, data fields and viewport controls while processing;
show Cancel at the bottom of the data panel. Report panel-specific status and
restore controls on completion, failure or cooperative cancellation. Keep the
last display on cancellation and retain the camera on successful replacement.
The component-job lifecycle is reusable for future fuselage/stabilizer builders.
